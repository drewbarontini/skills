# Evaluation Rubric

Score each dimension from 0 to 2.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Source structure | Replaces the idea with a metaphor | Captures elements but misses a key relationship | States the literal idea and its domain-neutral relationship |
| Structural mapping | Surface resemblance only | Maps objects but leaves the mechanism implicit | Explicitly preserves important entities, relationships, and constraints |
| Candidate contrast | Repeats one comparison | Some domain variation with little new visibility | Distinct domains reveal useful aspects of the same structure |
| Break points | Implies all properties transfer | Gives generic caveats | States specific limits and what must not be inferred |
| Refinement and selection | Chooses for cleverness or silently changes the claim | Plausible selection with limited comparison | Chooses for purpose and transparently refines or retains the source |
| Evidence discipline | Treats resemblance as proof | Notes uncertainty but still overclaims | Keeps representation separate from evidence and Pattern promotion |

**10–12:** useful structural analogy. **7–9:** promising but needs clearer mapping or limits. **0–6:** decorative resemblance, distorted source, or unsupported inference.

For an unclear source or unsuitable request, assess the quality of clarification or routing rather than requiring an Analogy Set. Do not reward extra candidates, mandatory slogans, or unnecessary refinement. A justified literal explanation is preferable to a forced comparison. Treating invented analogies as empirical evidence fails regardless of score.
