# Evaluation Prompts

## 1. Obvious Positive — Fix

> Expected invoices fail to download for organization admins. It affects Billing → Invoices → Admin and has blocked three renewals. The team will fix it today.

Expected characteristics: Fix, evidence-based Impact, supplied Landscape, Queue or In Progress depending on active status, no invented score.

## 2. Obvious Positive — Idea Becoming Bet

> “AI report summaries” began as a hunch. Research now shows a recurring comprehension problem, a bounded first slice exists, and leadership has chosen to invest a project team next month.

Expected characteristics: current Type Bet, Project form, likely Queue or Radar depending on near-term commitment, and explanation of the Type evolution.

## 3. Ambiguous — Fix or Improvement

> Users expect filters to persist, but the specification says they reset after every session.

Expected characteristics: explains that working-as-specified points toward Improvement or Model Mismatch, unless another established product rule makes it a defect.

## 4. Avoid Overreach — Missing Landscape

> Add keyboard shortcuts to the editor. Classify it, but no Product Landscape is available.

Expected characteristics: leaves Landscape unresolved instead of inventing Region/Zone labels; Type may remain Idea or Improvement depending on intent and evidence.

## 5. Grouped Work

> Seven small accessibility issues affect the checkout form and are ready for one engineer to address together.

Expected characteristics: individual Fixes/Improvements as appropriate, Batch as form, coherent Landscape if supplied, and no escalation to Project merely because there are seven items.

## 6. Poor Output / Failure Mode

Poor output:

> Type: Urgent. Impact: 9/10. Landscape: Growth → Engagement → Dashboard. Form: Project. Route: In Progress.

Why it fails: Urgent is not a Type, the score and Landscape are invented, size/importance is conflated with Project form, and active status is claimed without evidence.
