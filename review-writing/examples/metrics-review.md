# Example: Independent Writing Mirror review

This synthetic example illustrates the review boundary. The sample draft is not proposed prose for any real Work.

## Author's draft

> Teams measure response time because it is easy. A fast reply always means a better experience.
>
> Last month, we shortened our response time by half. Our customer satisfaction survey did not change.
>
> These number tell us that better metrics create better products.
>
> The dashboard looks healthier. The customers may not be.

## Writing Mirror

### Understanding

- **Central idea:** I read this as a caution against treating operational measurements as reliable proxies for customer outcomes.
- **Progression:** The piece begins with an assumption about speed, presents counterevidence, and closes by distinguishing dashboard improvement from customer benefit.
- **What lands:** The contrast in the final two sentences makes the measurement-versus-experience tension memorable.

### Clarity & Coherence

1. **Paragraph 3 — Potential friction**
   - **Evidence:** "better metrics create better products" follows a report that satisfaction "did not change."
   - **Observation:** The assertion appears to endorse the causal relationship the preceding evidence calls into question.
   - **Reader effect:** I am unsure whether the line is ironic or expresses the author's conclusion.
2. **Paragraph 1 — Potential friction**
   - **Evidence:** "A fast reply always means a better experience."
   - **Observation:** The absolute claim conflicts with the later example.
   - **Reader effect:** A reader may not know whether this is an assumption being challenged or a belief the author holds.

### Grammar & Mechanics

- **Paragraph 3 — Number agreement:** "These number" combines forms that do not agree in number.

### Open Questions

1. Is the first paragraph presenting a common assumption, or asserting the author's own position?
2. What relationship does the author intend between the unchanged survey result and the claim in paragraph 3?

## Boundary demonstrated

The review identifies exact friction and a grammatical issue. It does **not** provide corrected wording, propose a new transition, or instruct the author to add or remove content.
