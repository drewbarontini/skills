# Evaluation Rubric

Score each dimension from 0 to 2.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Inquiry and Assertion | Missing or unfalsifiable | Present but vague | Explicit, consequential, and revisable |
| Reality contact | Simulates completion or measures activity | Partial contact with relevant evidence | Direct, proportionate contact with relevant reality |
| Boundaries | Project-sized or arbitrary | Some limits | Small enough to run, with meaningful duration/scope/stop conditions |
| Signals | Vanity metric or invented precision | Relevant but incomplete | Observable, decision-relevant, and appropriately mixed |
| Learning map | Binary success theater | Maps two outcomes | Explains updates for positive, negative, and ambiguous outcomes |
| Experiment/Practice distinction | Conflates them | Names the distinction | Uses Experiment for learning and routes durable embodiment to Practice |

**10–12:** learning-ready. **7–9:** promising but needs sharper bounds or interpretation. **0–6:** activity plan, project, or habit mislabeled as an Experiment.
