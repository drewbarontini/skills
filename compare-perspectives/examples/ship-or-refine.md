# Ship or Refine

## Question and Stakes

Should a team release a useful but inconsistent new workflow now, or delay it to unify the experience first? Releasing creates earlier learning; delaying may protect product coherence.

## Perspectives

### Reality-Testing Lens

- Notices: assumptions remain unresolved until users encounter a complete slice.
- Assumes / values: feedback from reality is more truthful than prolonged simulation.
- Would protect or prioritize: a small release with fast observation.
- Blind spot or limit: may underweight seams that teach users conflicting concepts.

### Coherence Lens

- Notices: the workflow introduces terms and interactions that differ from similar parts of the product.
- Assumes / values: local value can weaken the whole when the user's mental model fragments.
- Would protect or prioritize: consistent concepts and behavior before broad exposure.
- Blind spot or limit: may mistake polish for essential coherence and delay learning.

### Strategic-Momentum Lens

- Notices: either a large release or an indefinite refinement cycle slows feedback.
- Assumes / values: small, complete iterations compound learning.
- Would protect or prioritize: the narrowest end-to-end slice that can be both coherent and real.
- Blind spot or limit: “small” can become a compromise that serves neither learning nor quality.

## Constructive Tensions

- Reality Testing and Coherence disagree about which uncertainty is riskier: market behavior or model fragmentation.
- Coherence and Momentum disagree about how much consistency is necessary before release.

## What the Tension Reveals

The decision is not simply speed versus quality. It turns on whether the inconsistency is cosmetic and reversible or conceptual and likely to teach the wrong mental model.

## Integrated Judgment

Release the smallest complete slice only after correcting conceptual inconsistencies with comparable workflows. Defer cosmetic refinement, observe real use immediately, and schedule a close comparison pass while the before-and-after remain easy to judge.

## Remaining Uncertainty

- Whether the remaining differences are perceived as seams by users.
- Whether the slice is complete enough to generate truthful behavior.
