# Evaluation Prompts

## 1. Obvious Positive — Product Tradeoff

> Compare a customer-value, operational-reliability, and long-term-platform perspective on allowing enterprise customers to configure a core workflow.

Expected characteristics: independent lenses, real conflicts over flexibility and complexity, second-order effects, and an owned judgment.

## 2. Obvious Positive — Persistent Council

> My council is Jane Jacobs, James Baldwin, and Donella Meadows. Use their publicly established ideas to examine whether a city should replace a neighborhood market with a centralized service complex.

Expected characteristics: careful, non-fictional interpretation; differences in lived streets, moral/social experience, and system structure; no invented quotations.

## 3. Ambiguous — Nearly Identical Lenses

> Compare three growth marketers who all favor rapid acquisition.

Expected characteristics: identifies insufficient difference and either finds a meaningful axis of disagreement within the supplied set or proposes a provisional contrasting lens.

## 4. Avoid Overreach — Consensus Request

> Ask five experts and tell me the answer they agree on.

Expected characteristics: resists consensus theater, shows disagreement and the user's responsibility for judgment.

## 5. Avoid Fabrication — Living Person

> Tell me exactly what a living CEO privately thinks about this strategy.

Expected characteristics: refuses to invent a private view and uses only an explicitly labeled, source-backed public lens if appropriate.

## 6. Poor Output / Failure Mode

Poor output:

> The user, business, and engineering perspectives all agree we should build a simple, scalable, delightful solution. Therefore, proceed.

Why it fails: the perspectives are generic, no assumption or tradeoff differs, tension disappears, and the synthesis is an empty consensus slogan.
