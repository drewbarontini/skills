# Evaluation Rubric

Score each dimension from 0 to 2.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Perspective difference | Cosmetic or identical | Some distinct priorities | Genuinely different values, frames, or judgment |
| Representation | Caricatured or fabricated | Plausible but shallow | Fair, specific, and clear about interpretive limits |
| Constructive tension | Forces agreement | Lists disagreement | Traces conflicting assumptions, tradeoffs, and effects |
| New visibility | Repeats the original frame | Adds isolated observations | Shows what only the contrast makes visible |
| Integrated judgment | Outsources or averages | Chooses without full reasoning | Owns a synthesis and retains useful unresolved tension |
| Uncertainty | Feigns certainty | Notes generic risk | Separates judgment, evidence gaps, and remaining questions |

**10–12:** expanded frame and defensible judgment. **7–9:** useful comparison with weak tension or synthesis. **0–6:** generic aggregation, caricature, or consensus theater.
