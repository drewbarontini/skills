# Onboarding Friction

## Input

> Trial users abandon workspace setup. We should add a skip button. Help us investigate.

## Current Inquiry

What prevents trial users from continuing through workspace setup, and how does that friction vary with what they expect to accomplish during their first session?

## Signal

Trial users abandon the product during workspace setup.

## Working Theory

The setup step may require information or commitment before users understand why it matters. A skip button is one possible response, not yet the problem definition.

## Assumptions and Evidence

- Assumption: the absence of a skip option causes abandonment.
- Evidence: abandonment occurs at the setup step; no causal evidence was provided.
- Unknown: what users expected, where they hesitated, and whether they lacked information, motivation, or a safe way to defer setup.

## Boundary

Trial users during their first product session, focused on the workspace-setup step.

## Next Move

Review several abandonment sessions and interview recent trial users about what they expected before and during the step.
