# Thinking Spaces

Choose the action first. Add Space or Mode only when it helps orient the Next Move.

Spaces describe the environment. Modes describe the engagement. Next Move describes the action.

**The Spaces hold the work. The Inquiry holds the state of the work:** Current Inquiry, Working Theory, and Next Move.

## Spaces

| Space | Function |
| --- | --- |
| Reading | Bring the world into thought: world → mind. |
| Writing | Bring thought into the world: mind → world. |
| Knowledge | Preserve understanding through time: understanding → continuity. |

Reading and Writing are conceptual names, not medium restrictions. A conversation, podcast, film, or artifact can serve Reading when engaged as material for thought. Outlining, mapping, sketching, or diagramming can serve Writing by externalizing thought.

Reading and Writing support cognitive work; Knowledge is the durable ledger. An Inquiry preserves the state of the work across Spaces rather than recording every temporary thought.

Sources identify material being engaged with. Working Material consists of what is extracted or created during that engagement, such as excerpts, quotes, and notes. Link relevant material to the Inquiry when it supports continuity; integrate understanding selectively rather than synchronizing every artifact into Knowledge. These are supporting materials, not additional stages or forms of Knowledge.

## Modes

- **Encounter** — bring in evidence, prior art, arguments, experience, and difference.
- **Articulate** — externalize and sharpen your own thinking.
- **Structure** — make hierarchy, relationships, tensions, systems, and causal structure visible.
- **Socialize** — expose thinking to another person's perspective, disagreement, experience, and judgment.
- **Challenge** — question, contrast, simulate, synthesize, and expose blind spots, including through AI.

Modes can operate within or across Spaces; they are not fixed pairings or KnowFlow stages.

## Durable Artifacts and Movement

Knowledge Space holds **Signal · Inquiry · Knowledge**:

- **Signal** preserves something meaningful enough to notice.
- **Inquiry** preserves the current state of something worth understanding.
- **Knowledge** preserves understanding durable enough to keep.

KnowFlow's **Notice → Question → Test → Understand → Express ↻** describes movement around these artifacts: Notice creates or sharpens a Signal; Question develops an Inquiry; Test expands, challenges, or revises it; Understand integrates learning into Knowledge; Express formalizes or embodies that Knowledge in the world.

**Pattern → Model → System** describes increasingly integrated forms within Knowledge.

An Experiment is a bounded test that provides contact with reality, a transition mechanism rather than a primary Knowledge Space artifact. When more cognitive work cannot resolve the uncertainty, make that test the Next Move without forcing it into a Space or designing the full Experiment. Expression is movement outward, not another stored knowledge object.

## Posture Is Distinct

- **Space** — Where am I thinking?
- **Mode** — How am I engaging?
- **Posture** — What stance am I taking?
- **Move** — What am I actually doing next?

Toolkit's Explorer, Integrator, and Editor Postures shape judgment across the work; they are not Thinking Modes. The Inquiry primarily records the Move. Space and Mode may orient it, while a Posture may shape judgment.
