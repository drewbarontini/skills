# Evaluation Rubric

Score each dimension from 0 to 2.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Signal fidelity | Rewrites observation as a cause | Partly separates observation and interpretation | Clearly preserves what was observed |
| Assumption discipline | Treats belief as fact | Names some assumptions | Separates assumptions, evidence, and unknowns |
| Solution independence | Embeds or validates a solution | Softens solution capture | Reopens the underlying problem without discarding useful intent |
| Focus | Too broad or trivial | Investigable with avoidable ambiguity | Open, bounded, consequential, and answerable through learning |
| Learning value | No concrete learning action or cannot update a belief | Action might add context but its learning purpose is unclear | Concrete, proportionate Next Move could materially update the Working Theory or next decision |
| Restraint | Answers the Inquiry or designs an unrequested full Experiment | Hints strongly at an answer or overdevelops the next step | Preserves uncertainty and proposes only the next learning move, including a bounded Experiment when reality contact is needed |

**10–12:** strong Inquiry. **7–9:** useful but needs reframing. **0–6:** premature answer, solution prompt, or unbounded topic.

When reviewing the Next Move, also check:

- Space and Mode may clarify the action, but labels never substitute for a concrete learning action. Do not penalize their omission when they add no value.
- Choose the action first; do not force every Inquiry into a Space or Mode, or favor continued cognitive work when a bounded Experiment would better test the Working Theory.
- Keep Spaces (Reading, Writing, Knowledge), Modes (Encounter, Articulate, Structure, Socialize, Challenge), KnowFlow movements, and Toolkit Postures distinct. Explorer, Integrator, and Editor are Postures, not Modes.
- Keep portable output centered on the Inquiry state and Next Move. Optional structured `Active Space` / `Active Mode` fields may be populated when clearly implied and available; they are not required Markdown headings.

A materially misleading orientation fails these checks even with a strong numerical score. These checks assess judgment, not taxonomy recitation or exact wording.
