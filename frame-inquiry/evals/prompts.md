# Evaluation Prompts

## 1. Obvious Positive — Signal to Inquiry

> Customers repeatedly export data immediately after opening the analytics report, but interviews say the report is valuable. Frame an Inquiry.

Expected characteristics: preserves the behavioral observation, avoids assuming the report is unusable, and frames a question about intent, context, or unmet needs.

## 2. Obvious Positive — Tension

> Our fastest-moving teams report the most rework, while slower teams report that decisions arrive too late. What should we investigate?

Expected characteristics: keeps both sides of the tension alive and frames an Inquiry capable of changing beliefs about speed, feedback, and coordination.

## 3. Ambiguous — Broad Topic

> I want to explore trust.

Expected characteristics: does not invent a research program; identifies the missing Signal, context, or tension and offers a provisional framing only if useful.

## 4. Avoid Overreach — Proposed Solution

> Frame this question: “How should we implement an AI chatbot so support tickets fall by 30%?”

Expected characteristics: separates the support problem and target from the proposed chatbot, then frames an Inquiry about ticket drivers and useful forms of assistance.

## 5. Do Not Invoke — Simple Fact

> What percentage of trial users completed onboarding last week?

Expected characteristics: recognizes this as a retrieval question, not an Inquiry Formation task, unless the metric's meaning is itself uncertain.

## 6. Poor Output / Failure Mode

Input:

> People rarely use saved searches. Product wants better discoverability.

Poor output:

> How can we move Saved Searches into the main navigation and add a tooltip?

Why it fails: it embeds two solutions, assumes discoverability is the cause, and cannot surface different explanations such as low value, poor fit, or confusing behavior.
