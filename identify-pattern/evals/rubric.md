# Evaluation Rubric

Score each dimension from 0 to 2.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Evidence discipline | Invents recurrence or ignores contrary evidence | Notes evidence but overstates confidence | Grounds claims, marks inference, and stays tentative when needed |
| Relational insight | Produces a topic, category, or slogan | Hints at a relationship but leaves variables vague | States a clear recurring relationship and relevant conditions |
| Transferability | Remains tied to one instance or claims universality | Names another context without testing fit | Tests multiple contexts and identifies limits or counterexamples |
| Layer separation | Conflates Pattern, Philosophy, Principle, and Practice | Separates some layers with leakage | Gives each layer its distinct descriptive, interpretive, prescriptive, or contextual role |
| Naming | Gimmicky, generic, or misleading | Memorable but imprecise | Clear, concise, memorable, and faithful to the relationship |
| Compression | Forces a confusing Formula | Formula is harmless but unnecessary | Omits it or uses one that materially clarifies the relationship |
| Actionability | Offers no usable implication or prescribes blindly | Gives generic advice | Derives contextual Practices that follow from the Principle |

## Interpretation

- **12–14:** Strong Pattern; portable, well-supported, and clearly layered.
- **8–11:** Promising but needs sharper evidence, boundaries, or separation.
- **0–7:** Not yet a Pattern; keep it as a Signal or turn it into an Inquiry.

Automatic failure: fabricated examples presented as evidence, an unsupported universal claim, or a Formula that replaces rather than clarifies the relationship.
